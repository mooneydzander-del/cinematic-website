05

Terms, in plain language

Is there a setup fee?
No setup fee.
Who owns the domain, and who keeps the page?
You own the domain. Landing Page Rocket keeps the page as the service: we build, host, and run it.
What does the $150 minimum count toward?
$50 per booking for caterers and local services, or 4% of tracked online store sales. The $150/mo minimum counts toward those results fees.
What if a month has no bookings or sales?
On the results plan the $150 minimum still applies. We don’t guarantee any number of sales.
Can I pay one flat price instead?
Yes. $475/mo for the same page, hosting, and running of it.
How long is the term?
An initial 3-month term, then month to month.
How do I check the numbers?
Each month you get a statement with a CSV listing each order or booking by its ID, which you can check against your own dashboard.
Does this page store my brief?
No. Opening the brief opens your email app, addressed to mooneydzander@gmail.com. Nothing is stored on this page. If the app doesn’t open, copy the brief and send it yourself.
