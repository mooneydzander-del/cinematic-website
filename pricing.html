Common Questions

Questions About Pricing and How It Works

What are the two offers and how are they priced?

Launch Your Landing Page is $1,000 to launch. Full Automation Launch is $1,500 to launch. Both are completed in 1 to 2 days, both include a free Month 2, and both continue into a monthly retainer after Month 3.

Why is Month 2 free?

Month 2 gives you time to send traffic to the page and see how the lead capture system works before the monthly retainer begins. You can see real visitors, real submissions, and real alerts before committing to the continuation phase.

What happens in Month 3?

Month 3 is the continuation phase. For Launch Your Landing Page, it is $2,000. For Full Automation Launch, it is $2,500. After Month 3, the recurring monthly retainer begins.

When does the monthly retainer start?

The recurring monthly retainer starts after Month 3. Launch Your Landing Page continues at $500/month. Full Automation Launch continues at $800/month. The retainer keeps the page hosted, maintained, updated, monitored, and supported.

What is the difference between the two offers?

Launch Your Landing Page is the focused option — a cinematic landing page connected to lead capture and instant alerts. Full Automation Launch adds deeper automation, booking flow support, automated confirmation emails, and a more complete lead handling system. The difference is how much the backend handles after the lead submits.

What does the monthly retainer cover?

The monthly retainer keeps the landing page live, hosted, and running. It covers maintenance, small updates, lead form monitoring, and ongoing support. The Full Automation Launch retainer also covers keeping the booking automation, confirmation emails, and follow-up workflows active. Neither plan includes ad management.

How fast can the page be launched?

Most landing pages are completed and launched in 1 to 2 days once the needed business information, offer details, and assets are provided.

Do you guarantee leads or sales?

No. No one can honestly guarantee exact leads or sales from a landing page alone. Results depend on the offer, ad traffic, market demand, follow-up speed, and sales process. Cinema builds the page, message, structure, and lead capture system to give your traffic a clearer path to action. That is what we control and what we focus on.

Why not just send ads to my regular website?

You can, but regular websites are built for general information — menus, about pages, service galleries, and multiple goals. A landing page is built around one offer and one action, which makes it significantly better for ad campaigns where you want visitors to do something specific. Every distraction you remove is a lead you keep.

Do I need to be running ads already?

No. Some businesses build the landing page first, then launch ads once the page is ready. Others are already running ads and want a better page to send that traffic to. Either situation works fine.
